# Office Joke — "Bring Your Whole Self"

One-line office joke entry for the [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) on make.fun.

## The joke

> HR told me to bring my whole self to work, so I brought my resignation too.

The setup borrows HR's own wellness slogan — "bring your whole self to work" — and the punchline takes it literally: the whole self includes the part that has already quit. One read, no explaining, no tired "meeting that could have been an email" cliché.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the single-line joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with a built-in check that the entry is 1–200 characters, a single non-blank line, per the competition gate.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out-office-suzy python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out-office-suzy/entry.txt
```
